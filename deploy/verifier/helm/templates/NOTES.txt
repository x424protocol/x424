Upgrades from 0.1.2 or earlier require a maintenance cutover: stop every old
verifier and protected-resource process, wait at least 15 minutes from the last
accepted old request, and only then deploy 0.1.3. Do not perform a rolling
upgrade. See docs/runbooks/state-namespace-0.1.3.md.

The chart deliberately runs one replica. World brokered handoff sessions are
process-local, so upgrades and restarts can return WORLD_SESSION_LOST for an
active handoff. Drain handoffs before changing the workload.

The evaluation NetworkPolicy permits DNS, outbound TCP 443, and outbound TCP
6379. prod-ha-0.2 rendering requires reviewed networkPolicy.dnsCidrs,
networkPolicy.worldCidrs, and networkPolicy.redisCidrs.

value only when that exact number of operator-controlled proxies is the sole
network path to this pod.
